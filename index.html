WOMEN IN STEM

Empowering women to break barriers, challenge expectations, share their ideas, and shape a more innovative future through STEM.

STEM is an important part of everyday life, from the technology we use to the scientific discoveries that improve our health and understanding of the world. However, women have historically been underrepresented in many STEM fields. This website was created to highlight women who challenged these barriers and made significant contributions to STEM.
